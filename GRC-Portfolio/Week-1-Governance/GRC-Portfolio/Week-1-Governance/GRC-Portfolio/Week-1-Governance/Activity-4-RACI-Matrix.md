1. Introduction
A RACI Matrix is a governance tool used to define roles and responsibilities for activities within an organisation.
RACI stands for:
•	R – Responsible: The person or team that carries out the task.
•	A – Accountable: The person who owns the task and makes sure it is completed. There should normally be one accountable role for each activity.
•	C – Consulted: People whose advice or expertise is needed before a decision or action is taken.
•	I – Informed: People who need to receive updates about the activity or its outcome.
VaultEdge currently has several governance weaknesses, including the absence of a dedicated GRC function, a vacant CISO position, informal security management, and limited security reporting.
This RACI Matrix proposes clear responsibilities to address these weaknesses and strengthen accountability.

4. Explanation of Key Responsibilities
Board of Directors
The Board provides strategic oversight of cybersecurity and governance. It approves the organisation's risk appetite and receives quarterly security reports.
CEO
The CEO provides executive leadership and ensures that security governance receives the resources and attention it requires. The CEO is accountable for the overall governance programme.
CISO
The CISO leads the organisation's information security programme, including security strategy, incident management, and security control oversight.
GRC Lead
The GRC Lead coordinates governance activities, risk assessments, compliance monitoring, policy development, and reporting. This role helps ensure that security activities are documented and aligned with business objectives.
CTO
The CTO is responsible for technical implementation, including infrastructure security, system access, engineering security practices, and technical remediation.
Business and Regional Leaders
These leaders ensure that employees and business units comply with approved policies. They also identify and report risks within their areas of responsibility.
Independent Assurance
Independent assurance reviews whether governance arrangements and controls are operating effectively. It should remain sufficiently independent from the teams responsible for implementing and operating those controls.
5. Governance Recommendations
Based on the VaultEdge case study, the following actions are recommended:
1.	Recruit the approved CISO to lead the information security programme.
2.	Establish a GRC function with a clearly defined reporting and oversight structure.
3.	Assign named owners to important security and compliance activities.
4.	Ensure the CTO is responsible for technical implementation while the CISO provides security leadership and oversight.
5.	Require business and regional leaders to take responsibility for security within their departments.
6.	Introduce quarterly security reporting to the Board, as required by the Board resolution.
7.	Review the RACI Matrix annually or whenever significant organisational changes occur.
8.	Ensure independent assurance does not take ownership of the controls it is expected to review.
6. Conclusion
The RACI Matrix provides VaultEdge with a proposed structure for assigning responsibility and accountability across its governance programme.
It helps address the organisation's current lack of clearly defined security responsibilities and reduces the risk of important tasks being ignored or duplicated.
By implementing this matrix, VaultEdge can improve coordination between the Board, executive management, security, technology, GRC, and business teams.
Clear accountability will also support the organisation's objective of achieving ISO 27001 certification within 12 months.
