1. Introduction
Governance Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are measurable values used to evaluate how effectively an organisation manages governance, risk, cybersecurity and regulatory compliance.
KPIs help management determine whether policies, procedures and security controls are working as intended.
VaultEdge Financial Technologies currently faces several governance and cybersecurity challenges, including:
•	No established GRC function.
•	A vacant CISO position.
•	Limited formal risk management processes.
•	Incomplete security policies and procedures.
•	Previous cybersecurity incidents involving exposed AWS credentials and phishing.
•	Twenty-three vendors without formal security assessments.
•	Former employees retaining access to company systems.
•	No formal employee security awareness programme.
•	Limited centralised security monitoring.
•	Customer requirements for ISO 27001 certification.
To address these challenges, VaultEdge needs measurable indicators that will help management monitor improvements and report progress to the Board.
This document proposes a governance KPI framework for measuring the effectiveness of the organisation's GRC programme.
2. Objectives of the Governance KPIs
The proposed KPIs will help VaultEdge:
1.	Measure the effectiveness of its governance programme.
2.	Monitor cybersecurity and enterprise risk exposure.
3.	Track regulatory and contractual compliance.
4.	Measure progress towards ISO 27001 certification.
5.	Monitor employee security awareness.
6.	Evaluate vendor security management.
7.	Improve accountability across departments.
8.	Provide meaningful security performance reports to the Board.
9.	Identify areas requiring corrective action.
3. Proposed Governance KPIs
The following KPIs are recommended for VaultEdge.
KPI 1: Security Policy Implementation Rate
What it measures: The percentage of required security policies that have been approved and implemented.
Why it matters: VaultEdge currently lacks several formal security policies, including acceptable use, password, data classification and access control policies.
Calculation:
(Number of approved and implemented required policies ÷ Total required policies) × 100
Proposed Target: 100% of identified priority security policies approved and implemented within six months.
Measurement Frequency: Monthly.
Responsible Owner: GRC Lead, with CISO oversight.
KPI 2: High-Risk Remediation Rate
What it measures: The percentage of identified high-risk security findings remediated within their approved deadlines.
Why it matters: VaultEdge needs to improve its ability to identify and correct vulnerabilities and other significant security weaknesses.
Calculation:
(Number of high-risk findings remediated within deadline ÷ Total high-risk findings due for remediation) × 100
Proposed Target: At least 90%.
Measurement Frequency: Monthly.
Responsible Owner: CISO and CTO.
KPI 3: Employee Security Awareness Completion Rate
What it measures: The percentage of employees who complete mandatory security awareness training.
Why it matters: VaultEdge previously experienced a phishing incident involving an employee who entered credentials into a fraudulent HR password reset page.
Calculation:
(Number of employees who completed security awareness training ÷ Total employees required to complete training) × 100
Proposed Target: At least 98% completion.
Measurement Frequency: Monthly.
Responsible Owner: CISO and Human Resources.
KPI 4: Access Review Completion Rate
What it measures: The percentage of scheduled user access reviews completed within the required period.
Why it matters: VaultEdge has identified former employees who retained access to Slack, GitHub and Jira after leaving the organisation.
Calculation:
(Number of scheduled access reviews completed ÷ Total scheduled access reviews) × 100
Proposed Target: 100% of scheduled quarterly access reviews completed.
Measurement Frequency: Quarterly.
Responsible Owner: CTO and relevant system owners, with CISO oversight.
KPI 5: Vendor Security Assessment Coverage
What it measures: The percentage of active vendors assessed for security risks according to their level of access and importance.
Why it matters: VaultEdge currently has 23 vendors that have not undergone formal security assessments.
Calculation:
(Number of active vendors assessed ÷ Total active vendors requiring assessment) × 100
Proposed Target: 100% of critical and high-risk vendors assessed within six months, followed by a risk-based assessment programme for other vendors.
Measurement Frequency: Quarterly.
Responsible Owner: GRC Lead and relevant vendor relationship owners.
KPI 6: Security Incident Response Time
What it measures: The time taken to acknowledge and begin responding to reported security incidents.
Why it matters: VaultEdge's previous AWS credential exposure incident involved delays between discovery, notification and credential rotation.
Calculation:
Average time between incident reporting and initial acknowledgement or response.
Proposed Target: At least 95% of reported high-severity incidents acknowledged within 30 minutes, subject to an approved incident severity and response policy.
Measurement Frequency: Monthly.
Responsible Owner: CISO and Security Operations Team.
KPI 7: Regulatory Compliance Assessment Coverage
What it measures: The percentage of identified applicable regulatory and contractual obligations that have been assessed and assigned an owner.
Why it matters: VaultEdge has not conducted formal compliance assessments across its operating jurisdictions.
Calculation:
(Number of identified applicable obligations assessed and assigned an owner ÷ Total identified applicable obligations) × 100
Proposed Target: 100% of identified applicable obligations assessed and assigned an owner within six months.
Measurement Frequency: Quarterly.
Responsible Owner: GRC Lead, with legal and privacy support.
KPI 8: ISO 27001 Implementation Progress
What it measures: Progress against the approved ISO 27001 implementation plan.
Why it matters: Major customers require ISO 27001 certification or equivalent assurance, and VaultEdge has a target of achieving certification within 12 months.
Calculation:
(Number of planned implementation milestones completed on time ÷ Total implementation milestones due) × 100
Proposed Target: At least 90% of planned milestones completed on schedule, with the overall objective of achieving certification within 12 months.
Measurement Frequency: Monthly.
Responsible Owner: CISO and GRC Lead.
KPI 9: Security Incident Post-Incident Review Completion
What it measures: The percentage of significant security incidents that receive a documented post-incident review.
Why it matters: VaultEdge needs to improve incident documentation, root cause analysis and lessons learned.
Calculation:
(Number of significant incidents with completed post-incident reviews ÷ Total significant incidents requiring review) × 100
Proposed Target: 100%.
Measurement Frequency: Quarterly.
Responsible Owner: CISO and GRC Lead.
KPI 10: Governance Action Closure Rate
What it measures: The percentage of governance and security improvement actions completed by their agreed deadlines.
Why it matters: Governance meetings must lead to measurable improvements rather than discussions without follow-up.
Calculation:
(Number of governance actions completed by their due dates ÷ Total governance actions due) × 100
Proposed Target: At least 90%.
Measurement Frequency: Monthly.
Responsible Owner: GRC Lead, with action owners responsible for completing assigned tasks.

5. KPI Reporting and Governance
The proposed KPIs should be monitored by their respective owners and consolidated by the GRC function.
The following reporting arrangements are recommended:
Weekly:
•	Review high-risk security findings.
•	Monitor significant incidents and outstanding technical actions.
Monthly:
•	Consolidate governance KPI results.
•	Review policy implementation and compliance progress.
•	Track risk remediation and governance actions.
•	Present performance to the Executive Risk and Governance Committee.
Quarterly:
•	Present the governance KPI dashboard to the Board.
•	Review significant trends and outstanding risks.
•	Identify areas requiring additional resources or management decisions.
6. KPI Performance Management
VaultEdge should use the following performance categories to communicate KPI results.
Performance Status	Meaning	Recommended Action
Green	Target achieved	Maintain performance and continue monitoring.
Amber	Target not achieved but performance is close	Investigate the gap and introduce corrective action.
Red	Significant failure to meet target	Escalate to management and agree urgent corrective action.
The organisation should establish clear thresholds for each KPI. These should be approved by management and reviewed as the governance programme matures.
7. Implementation Recommendations
To implement the proposed KPI framework, VaultEdge should:
1.	Appoint the approved CISO.
2.	Establish the GRC function and assign KPI coordination responsibilities.
3.	Confirm the final KPI definitions and targets with management.
4.	Assign a named owner to every KPI.
5.	Establish reliable data collection and reporting processes.
6.	Create a monthly governance KPI dashboard.
7.	Review KPI performance during executive governance meetings.
8.	Present quarterly KPI results to the Board.
9.	Investigate missed targets and document corrective actions.
10.	Review KPI relevance and effectiveness annually.
8. Conclusion
The proposed governance KPIs will provide VaultEdge with measurable indicators for evaluating its governance, cybersecurity and compliance performance.
By monitoring policy implementation, risk remediation, employee awareness, access reviews, vendor assessments and ISO 27001 progress, the organisation can identify weaknesses and measure improvement.
Regular reporting will also provide the Board and executive management with better visibility into security performance and outstanding risks.
These indicators will help VaultEdge establish a more accountable, measurable and continually improving governance programme.
