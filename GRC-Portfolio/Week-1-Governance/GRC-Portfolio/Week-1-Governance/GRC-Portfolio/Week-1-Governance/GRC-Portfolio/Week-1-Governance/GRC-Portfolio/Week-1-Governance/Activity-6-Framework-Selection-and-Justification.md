1. Introduction
Cybersecurity and IT governance frameworks provide organisations with structured guidance for managing technology, information security, risk and regulatory compliance.
VaultEdge Financial Technologies operates in the fintech industry and handles sensitive customer information, financial transactions and banking-related services.
The organisation currently faces several challenges, including:
•	No established GRC function.
•	A vacant Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) position.
•	Limited formal risk management processes.
•	No comprehensive information security policy framework.
•	Previous cybersecurity incidents involving exposed AWS credentials and phishing.
•	Twenty-three vendors without formal security assessments.
•	Regulatory obligations across multiple countries.
•	Customer requirements for ISO 27001 certification or SOC 2 Type II evidence.
To address these challenges, VaultEdge needs recognised frameworks that support governance, risk management, information security and compliance.
This document evaluates relevant frameworks and recommends an appropriate combination for the organisation.
2. Frameworks Considered
The following frameworks and standards have been considered:
1.	ISO/IEC 27001
2.	NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
3.	COBIT
4.	COSO Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
5.	ISO 31000
6.	ITIL
3. Framework Evaluation and Justification
3.1 ISO/IEC 27001 – Information Security Management System
Purpose:
ISO/IEC 27001 provides requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
How it helps VaultEdge:
•	Establishes a structured information security management programme.
•	Supports identification and treatment of information security risks.
•	Provides a foundation for security policies and procedures.
•	Helps protect customer information and business systems.
•	Supports customer and investor confidence.
•	Provides a certification route recognised internationally.
Why it is important:
VaultEdge has major customers requesting ISO 27001 certification. Meridian Bank requires certification by its renewal in 10 months, while other customers have similar requirements.
Recommendation: HIGH PRIORITY – Adopt ISO/IEC 27001 as the primary information security management standard.
3.2 NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
Purpose:
The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides guidance for understanding, assessing, prioritising and managing cybersecurity risks.
Its six functions in CSF 2.0 are:
•	Govern
•	Identify
•	Protect
•	Detect
•	Respond
•	Recover
How it helps VaultEdge:
•	Helps establish cybersecurity governance.
•	Supports identification of critical assets and risks.
•	Guides implementation of preventive security controls.
•	Improves threat detection and incident response.
•	Supports recovery and business resilience.
•	Provides a practical structure for measuring cybersecurity maturity.
Why it is important:
VaultEdge has experienced credential exposure and phishing incidents. It also lacks centralised security monitoring and formal incident response arrangements.
The NIST CSF provides a useful structure for improving these areas.
Recommendation: HIGH PRIORITY – Use NIST CSF 2.0 to organise and improve the cybersecurity programme.
3.3 COBIT – IT Governance and Management
Purpose:
COBIT provides a framework for the governance and management of enterprise information and technology.
How it helps VaultEdge:
•	Defines accountability for IT governance.
•	Aligns technology activities with business objectives.
•	Improves management oversight.
•	Supports performance measurement.
•	Helps establish clear governance processes.
•	Strengthens accountability between executives, technology teams and business units.
Why it is important:
VaultEdge currently has informal security management, unclear responsibilities and limited governance oversight.
COBIT can help establish stronger IT governance and management arrangements.
Recommendation: MEDIUM PRIORITY – Use relevant COBIT practices to strengthen IT governance and accountability.
3.4 COSO Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
Purpose:
COSO ERM provides principles and a framework for integrating risk management with strategy, performance and organisational objectives.
How it helps VaultEdge:
•	Connects risk management with business strategy.
•	Supports executive and board-level risk oversight.
•	Encourages risk-aware decision-making.
•	Helps management understand the effect of risks on business objectives.
•	Supports enterprise-wide risk management.
Why it is important:
VaultEdge is preparing for future investment and business expansion. It needs a structured approach to understanding risks that could affect its growth, revenue and reputation.
Recommendation: MEDIUM PRIORITY – Consider COSO ERM as the organisation develops its enterprise-wide risk management programme.
3.5 ISO 31000 – Risk Management Guidelines
Purpose:
ISO 31000 provides principles and guidelines for managing risk across an organisation.
How it helps VaultEdge:
•	Provides a structured approach to identifying risks.
•	Supports risk analysis and evaluation.
•	Guides risk treatment decisions.
•	Encourages continuous risk monitoring and communication.
•	Can be applied across technology, finance, operations and compliance.
Why it is important:
VaultEdge has no formal risk management process. ISO 31000 can help establish a consistent approach to identifying and managing risks across the organisation.
Recommendation: MEDIUM PRIORITY – Use ISO 31000 as a supporting risk management guideline.
3.6 ITIL – IT Service Management
Purpose:
ITIL provides guidance for managing IT-enabled services and improving service delivery.
How it helps VaultEdge:
•	Improves IT service management.
•	Supports incident and problem management.
•	Encourages structured change management.
•	Helps improve service availability and reliability.
•	Supports continual service improvement.
Why it is important:
VaultEdge has weaknesses in change management, and developers sometimes push changes into production without approval.
ITIL practices can help introduce more controlled IT service and change management processes.
Recommendation: LOWER INITIAL PRIORITY – Adopt relevant ITIL practices as IT service management processes mature.

5. Recommended Framework Adoption Strategy
VaultEdge should avoid trying to implement every framework at the same time. A phased approach will help the organisation manage costs, resources and implementation workload.
Phase 1: Immediate Priorities – Months 1–3
1.	Establish the GRC function and recruit the approved CISO.
2.	Begin ISO 27001 gap assessment and ISMS planning.
3.	Use NIST CSF 2.0 to assess current cybersecurity capabilities.
4.	Establish a risk register and risk assessment process.
5.	Prioritise critical security weaknesses and regulatory obligations.
Phase 2: Implementation – Months 4–8
1.	Develop and implement information security policies.
2.	Introduce risk treatment plans and control ownership.
3.	Strengthen access management and security monitoring.
4.	Improve incident response and business continuity arrangements.
5.	Conduct vendor risk assessments.
6.	Implement relevant COBIT and ISO 31000 practices.
Phase 3: Assurance and Improvement – Months 9–12
1.	Conduct internal ISMS audits.
2.	Address identified nonconformities.
3.	Perform management reviews.
4.	Prepare for the ISO 27001 certification audit.
5.	Review cybersecurity performance and governance KPIs.
6.	Identify areas for continual improvement.
Note: The proposed phases are a planning recommendation, not a guarantee of certification within 12 months. Actual timing will depend on the gap assessment, available resources, implementation progress and audit findings.
6. Regulatory and Customer Alignment
The selected frameworks support VaultEdge's broader compliance and customer requirements.
Requirement	Relevant Framework or Standard	Reason
Customer demand for ISO certification	ISO/IEC 27001	Provides a certifiable information security management standard.
Card payment security	PCI DSS	Provides security requirements relevant to organisations handling payment card data.
EU personal data protection	GDPR	Provides applicable legal obligations for processing personal data.
UK financial services expectations	Applicable FCA requirements and guidance	Supports consideration of relevant regulatory expectations.
Kenya personal data protection	Kenya Data Protection Act	Provides applicable data protection obligations.
Singapore personal data protection	Singapore PDPA	Provides applicable personal data protection obligations.
Important distinction: ISO 27001, NIST CSF, COBIT, COSO, ISO 31000 and ITIL are frameworks or standards. GDPR, PCI DSS, the Kenya Data Protection Act and Singapore PDPA are legal or industry compliance requirements. Adopting a framework does not automatically establish compliance with these requirements.
7. Final Recommendations
Based on VaultEdge's business model, customer requirements and current security weaknesses, the following recommendations are proposed:
1.	Adopt ISO/IEC 27001 as the primary information security management standard.
2.	Use NIST CSF 2.0 to structure cybersecurity risk management and capability improvement.
3.	Use COBIT practices to strengthen IT governance and accountability.
4.	Apply ISO 31000 guidelines to support consistent risk management.
5.	Consider COSO ERM as the enterprise risk management programme develops.
6.	Introduce relevant ITIL practices to improve change, incident and service management.
7.	Review the effectiveness of the selected frameworks regularly.
8. Conclusion
The selection of appropriate frameworks will help VaultEdge establish a more structured approach to governance, risk management, information security and IT service delivery.
ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST CSF 2.0 should receive the highest initial priority because they directly address the organisation's information security weaknesses and customer expectations.
Other frameworks can provide additional support as the governance programme matures.
By adopting these frameworks through a phased implementation strategy, VaultEdge can improve accountability, strengthen its security posture, manage regulatory obligations and work towards its ISO 27001 certification objective.
